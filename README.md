# AI Business Stack: 10 Open-Source Repos

A curated list of open-source GitHub projects for going from an idea to a real AI business. Combine them to build a full ecosystem. Source: a post by @syntaix.ai on Instagram.

**Note:** open-source does not mean zero cost. Hosting, AI APIs and other services may require payment.

| # | Project | What it does | Repo |
|---|---------|--------------|------|
| 1 | Saasfly | Build your own SaaS product (Next.js boilerplate) | [saasfly/saasfly](https://github.com/saasfly/saasfly) |
| 2 | R2R | Turn business documents into AI knowledge (RAG) | [SciPhi-AI/R2R](https://github.com/SciPhi-AI/R2R) |
| 3 | Scira | Build AI-powered search experiences | [zaidmukaddam/scira](https://github.com/zaidmukaddam/scira) |
| 4 | Lago | Usage-based billing for your AI product | [getlago/lago](https://github.com/getlago/lago) |
| 5 | Marketing Skills | Marketing workflows for your AI coding assistant | [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) |
| 6 | Google Maps Scraper | Find potential customers | [gosom/google-maps-scraper](https://github.com/gosom/google-maps-scraper) |
| 7 | Frappe CRM | Manage leads and sales | [frappe/crm](https://github.com/frappe/crm) |
| 8 | Dittofeed | Automate customer emails and messaging | [dittofeed/dittofeed](https://github.com/dittofeed/dittofeed) |
| 9 | Papermark | Share proposals and track views | [mfts/papermark](https://github.com/mfts/papermark) |
| 10 | Counterscale | Track website traffic and referrals | [benvinegar/counterscale](https://github.com/benvinegar/counterscale) |

## How they fit together

**Build:** Saasfly (app), R2R (knowledge), Scira (search)

**Monetize:** Lago (billing)

**Grow:** Marketing Skills, Google Maps Scraper (leads), Frappe CRM (pipeline), Dittofeed (email), Papermark (proposals), Counterscale (analytics)

Check each repo's license before commercial use (Lago is AGPLv3).
